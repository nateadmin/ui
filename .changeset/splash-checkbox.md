New SplashCheckbox: a round checkbox that splashes in the accent color when ticked (from Uiverse.io by Shoh2008, MIT). Boxes that load checked show filled without replaying the splash, and clicks don't reach the row underneath. Its styles ship in styles.css under `.sf-cte`; the tick uses the theme's on-accent color so it shows in dark mode. The catalog has a new Checkbox page comparing it with the simple round ListCheckbox.
