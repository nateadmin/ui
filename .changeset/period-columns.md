Period menu in three columns: Days | Weeks and Months | Quarters and Years, with rolling ranges (24h, 3 to 120 days, 1 to 4 weeks, 3, 6, 12 months) and named quarters and years. Presets take `group`, `column`, and a label worked out from today. The field keeps the name of the period picked when two cover the same days.
