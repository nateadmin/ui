ListPage: a whole list page as one component. The bar (Search, filters, Filters, Export, the page's actions; icons on a phone), the list (sorting, rows that open, bulk select, cards on a phone) and the footer. Hand it every row and it sorts and pages them itself, or hand it one page with a total for a list the server pages. An app supplies rows, columns, filter choices and handlers and nothing else.
