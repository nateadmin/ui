ReportGridColumns: the column-title row with its behavior built in. Click a title to sort, drag a title to move its column, and a line down the whole column shows where it will land while the moved column fades. It was written inside the catalog's Report page, so apps did not get it; the page now uses the component. The Total band keeps one height with or without the sideways scrollbar, so its text is always centered.
