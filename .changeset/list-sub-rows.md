DataList: sub-rows. `children(row)` gives the rows under a row, drawn in the same columns, indented, folded by a chevron on the parent; the header offers Expand all / Collapse all; `openRows`/`onOpenRowsChange` or `defaultOpen` control what starts open. Sub-rows count for column widths and cannot be selected.
