New DataList: the list under a toolbar, built from columns, with cell parts (ListTitle, ListBadge, ListNumber, ListDate, ListMono, ListMuted, ListNone) and sortRows(). The catalog's List page opens with a builder: turn on any of 27 field types and the row parts, pick row end, row height, title style and layout, and drag columns into order, over 2,000 placeholder rows.
